- **Voice:** speech recognition and spoken replies run **on your phone**; **audio is never sent**. **"AI help"** (on by default): when the assistant can't understand a phrase, the **text** of that phrase, with a little context, may go to our Australian server and to **OpenAI (USA)** to work it out. If it still can't after three tries, the conversation so far is sent as text (never more than a few kilobytes). Turn "AI help" off in Settings and everything stays on your phone.
- **Support:** you can call us from the app, **Monday–Friday 9 am–5 pm (Canberra time)**. We keep a short record of each call (who, when, who helped, the outcome), not the call itself.
- **Your books:** the income and expenses you record in AURA, and the people you record in them, are stored on our Australian servers; the books **don't store receipt photos**. **People you record (names, relationship, e.g. dependants) are other people's information; add only what you need.** Your **Contact Book** is also kept on our Australian servers; it's other people's information too, so add only what you need.
- **Calls:** one-to-one calls go directly between the two phones and are end-to-end encrypted; only if a network blocks a direct link does the call go through our relay server in Australia, which can't decrypt it. In a direct call, each phone can see the other's internet (IP) address, as with any direct call; it can show a rough location. Call only people you trust.
- **Your QR code:** when someone scans your AURA QR code, your two phones link through an encrypted handshake. If your account has a phone number, it is sent to them in that handshake (we only pass on the sealed packet and can't read it; the number isn't in the code image) and saved in their Contact Book, which is backed up on our servers with their account, like a number they typed. It only fills their phone field if it is empty. Each code works once, for 2 minutes; a new one appears after each scan. Removing someone from your contacts **doesn't** remove your number from theirs. If you don't want your number shared, only show your code to people you want to have it, or sign up with email instead (a number used to sign up can't be removed in the app). We never read or change your phone's own address book.
- **Demos:** rides, hotels, food, the marketplace and MAVEN venues use **sample data**. Nothing is booked, bought or delivered, and no money changes hands. A demo route you try is used to draw the map and **isn't stored**.
- **How we collect it:** from you, when you sign up and use the app. We don't buy data, and we don't read or upload your phone's address book.
- **If you don't give it:** we need your name and a phone number or email to run an account. Location and crash reports are optional; without location the demo map can't show where you are.
- We **don't sell** personal information, show ads or track you across apps. We don't use automated decisions that significantly affect you.
