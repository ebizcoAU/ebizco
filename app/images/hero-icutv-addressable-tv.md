## Size

1920 x 1080 px (16:9 widescreen), PNG or JPG. The image is displayed full-bleed behind the hero and cropped with `object-fit: cover`, so keep the main subject within the center-safe area (avoid placing anything critical within ~5% of the top/bottom/left/right edges).

## Description

Photorealistic, wide-format hero image for an addressable-TV advertising SaaS platform. A modern living room at dusk, minimalist Scandinavian-style interior, softly blurred and dim on the left. On the right side of the frame, a large flat-screen TV displays a subtly abstract ad-targeting visual — soft glowing rings/circles converging toward the center of the screen, suggesting precision targeting — with warm ambient lamp light spilling into the room.

**Composition:** TV screen positioned in the right two-thirds of the frame as the focal point; comfortable living space fading into soft, dim, uncluttered space on the left third. The left ~40% will be darkened further by a CSS gradient and hold the headline text, so keep that area visually calm.

**Style:** premium B2B SaaS aesthetic, warm interior lighting contrasted with a cool blue glow from the screen, clean modern interior design, 4K quality. No visible logos, brand names, or readable text anywhere in the image (including on the TV screen).

**Mood:** precise, modern, effective advertising technology.
