# hero-antifake-authentication

- **Used on:** Homepage hero, Slide 2 (AntiFake)
- **Target file:** `src/assets/img/others/hero-antifake-authentication.png`
- **Once generated, update:** `web/app/page.js` → `heroSlides[1].image` → `/assets/img/others/hero-antifake-authentication.png` (and copy the file into `web/public/assets/img/others/` too, so the dev/build server can serve it)

## Size

1920 x 1080 px (16:9 widescreen), PNG or JPG. The image is displayed full-bleed behind the hero and cropped with `object-fit: cover`, so keep the main subject within the center-safe area (avoid placing anything critical within ~5% of the top/bottom/left/right edges).

## Description

Photorealistic, wide-format hero image for a product-authentication technology website. Close-up macro shot of a premium product package (a plain, unbranded pharmaceutical or luxury-goods box) resting on a clean dark surface, softly lit and fading into darkness on the left. On the right side of the frame, a smartphone hovers over the packaging mid-scan, a glowing holographic checkmark / verification badge rising from the scan point above the QR code or NFC tag on the box.

**Composition:** product box and scanning phone positioned in the right two-thirds of the frame; the left third stays dark and simple. The left ~40% will be darkened further by a CSS gradient and hold the headline text, so keep that area visually calm.

**Style:** premium B2B SaaS aesthetic, dark background with deep-blue and teal accent glow, sharp macro focus on the verification moment, 4K quality. No visible logos, brand names, or readable text on the packaging or UI.

**Mood:** trustworthy, secure, high-tech anti-counterfeiting.
