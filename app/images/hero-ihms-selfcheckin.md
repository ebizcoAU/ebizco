- **Used on:** Homepage hero, Slide 1 (iHMS / OZLODGE)

- **Once generated, update:** `web/app/page.js` → `heroSlides[0].image` → `/assets/img/others/hero-ihms-selfcheckin.png` (and copy the file into `web/public/assets/img/others/` too, so the dev/build server can serve it)

## Size

1920 x 1080 px (16:9 widescreen), PNG or JPG. The image is displayed full-bleed behind the hero and cropped with `object-fit: cover`, so keep the main subject within the center-safe area (avoid placing anything critical within ~5% of the top/bottom/left/right edges).

## Description

Photorealistic, wide-format hero image for a hospitality technology website. A modern boutique hotel corridor at dusk with warm ambient lighting, receding into soft focus on the left. On the right side of the frame: an elegant guest-room door fitted with a sleek smart lock — a subtle backlit keypad/NFC reader, no visible brand logo on the lock — with a guest's hand holding a smartphone displaying a minimalist digital room-key / check-in app screen (abstract UI, no readable text), about to tap the lock to unlock it.

**Composition:** main subject (smart lock + phone) in the right two-thirds of the frame; corridor fading into soft, dim, uncluttered space on the left third. The left ~40% will be darkened further by a CSS gradient and hold the headline text, so keep that area visually calm.

**Style:** premium B2B SaaS aesthetic, architectural interior photography, deep blue and warm gold accent lighting, clean modern hotel interior design, shallow depth of field, 4K quality. No visible logos, brand names, or readable text anywhere in the image.

**Mood:** confident, seamless, technologically advanced hospitality experience.
